# Opening Story: The Crossroads Waystation

The cold mountain wind howls across the jagged pass of the Ironridge Range, carrying the sharp scent of cedar and impending snow. Before you stands the heavy oak door of the **Crossroads Waystation**, its iron lanterns flickering warmly against the twilight.

Inside, the tavern hums with low murmurs, the crackle of a roaring stone hearth, and the rhythmic clinking of flagons. Travelers, mercenaries, and cloaked wanderers huddle around weathered tables, their eyes darting toward each newcomer.

Behind the polished timber bar stands Oakhaven, the scarred barkeep, wiping a wooden mug with a dry cloth. He glances up as you shake the frost from your cloak.

"Welcome to the Crossroads, stranger," Oakhaven rumbles, nodding toward an empty stool by the fire. "Rest your boots, check your pack, and steady your nerves. The mountain roads aren't kind to the unprepared."

You take a seat by the crackling embers and assess your situation.

### 🎲 GetoptAID Sandbox Command Center

This scenario is running **GetoptAID**! You can type any of the following slash commands into the action bar (in **Do**, **Say**, or **Story** mode):

#### 1. Dice Roller (`/roll` or `/r`)
* `/roll -d 20` — Roll a standard 20-sided die.
* `/roll -d 20 -m 4` — Roll d20 with a +4 modifier.
* `/roll -c 3 -d 6` — Roll three 6-sided dice.
* `/roll -d 20 -a` — Roll with advantage (rolls twice, takes highest).
* `You say, "/roll -d 20 -m 2."` — Dialogue quoting works automatically!
* `/roll —d 20` — Mobile em-dashes (`—`) are normalized seamlessly.

#### 2. Inventory Manager (`/inv` or `/i`)
* `/inv -l` — List your current inventory, equipped gear, and gold.
* `/inv -a "Steel Dagger" -a "Healing Draught"` — Add multiple items (array accumulator).
* `/inv -g 50` — Add 50 gold coins (`/inv -g -15` to spend).
* `/inv -e "Steel Dagger"` — Equip an item from your pack.
* `/inv -d "Healing Draught"` — Drop an item.

#### 3. Character Status (`/stat` or `/s`)
* `/stat -v` — View your character sheet, attributes, and tags.
* `/stat --hp 85 --mp 40` — Update health and mana pools.
* `/stat -t "stealthy" -t "bilingual"` — Add character trait tags.
* `/stat --meta class=Ranger --meta level=3` — Assign custom key-value metadata.

#### 4. Help & Command Reference
* `/help` (or `/help roll`, `/help inv`) — Display command manuals.
